# Provider outreach campaign: prompt and checklist

For whoever is making the campaign (no marketing experience needed). Read "Before you start", then paste the prompt in the box
below into an AI tool (Claude, ChatGPT, or Canva's "Magic Design") and follow "What to do with what you get back".

---

## Before you start (10 minutes, please do not skip)

**Who we are writing to.** Businesses that are already listed on the *public* NDIS Commission or My Aged Care registers and publish a
contact email on their own website. We are inviting them to claim a free SolDirectory listing. They have not asked to hear from us, so
Australian law (the Spam Act 2003) has three rules that always apply:

1. **Say who we are.** Every email names Sol Business Consultant Pty Ltd (ABN 20 662 022 522) and gives the street address
   (97 John St, Glenroy VIC 3046).
2. **A working unsubscribe link in every email.** Mailchimp adds it with the merge tag `*|UNSUB|*`. Anyone who unsubscribes is never
   emailed again.
3. **Only write about something relevant to their business.** This is an invitation to a free listing in a provider directory, which is.
   It is not a general advertisement.

**Only use the list we give you.** The file `outreach-verified-<date>.csv` contains only businesses that have not joined, have not opted
out, and whose email was found on their own website. Do not add addresses from anywhere else.

**Be honest about what we offer.** Do not promise customers, leads, rankings, or income. Do not use the words "unclaimed", "guaranteed",
"verified by the NDIS", "approved by the NDIS", or "official partner". We are independent: not part of, or endorsed by, the NDIA, the NDIS
Commission or My Aged Care.

**One thing to know about Mailchimp.** Mailchimp's rules say a list should be made of people who agreed to hear from you, and they can
pause an account that sends to a cold list or sees many spam complaints. To keep the account safe: send small batches (200 to 300 a day for the
first week, then increase), send from a real domain address with a real reply-to, keep the email short and plain, and stop and tell us if
unsubscribes go over about 2 in 100 or Mailchimp sends a warning. If Mailchimp refuses the list, tell us: there are other ways to send it.

**Before every new campaign:** export Mailchimp's "Unsubscribed" and "Cleaned" contacts and send them to us so we can add them to our
do-not-email list.

## The prompt to paste

```
You are a friendly senior marketing designer helping a beginner. Create a short email campaign and matching social graphics that invite
disability and aged care businesses in Australia to claim a FREE listing on SolDirectory. Give me exact text, exact design instructions and
everything I need to build it in Canva and Mailchimp. Use plain language, no jargon. Do not invent facts: use ONLY the facts below.

ABOUT SOLDIRECTORY (the only facts you may use)
- SolDirectory (directory.solbusinessconsultant.com.au) is a free provider directory where people find NDIS and aged care providers.
  It is run by Sol Business Consultant Pty Ltd, Glenroy, Victoria.
- Families and participants use it for free. They tell us what support they need once, and providers who have recently confirmed they have
  capacity can respond. Each request is shared with a small number of providers (up to 3 can open it), and the person chooses who to speak to.
- Providers confirm their availability every week, so the directory only shows who is taking new people.
- Paying never moves a provider higher in the results. Results are not ordered by payment.
- Every business on the public NDIS Commission and My Aged Care registers already has a basic page on SolDirectory showing what the
  register publishes. A business can claim its page for free to manage its details and receive requests.
- Claiming is free. The page to start is: https://directory.solbusinessconsultant.com.au/providers
- Contact: directory@solbusinessconsultant.com.au or 0460 003 494.
- SolDirectory is independent. It is not part of, or endorsed by, the NDIA, the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission or My Aged Care.

DO NOT SAY (strict)
- No promises of customers, leads, income, "more clients", rankings or results. No numbers about how many families use it.
- No testimonials, reviews, quotes, awards or logos of other organisations.
- Never use the words: unclaimed, guaranteed, verified by the NDIS, approved, official partner, best, number one.
- Do not use the NDIS logo, My Aged Care logo or any government logo or colours.

BRAND
- Colours: navy #0B2D5C, bright blue #1769E0, pale background #F5F8FC, white, dark text #1B2B43. Nothing else except tints of these.
- Font: Barlow (free in Canva and Google Fonts). Headlines bold, body regular.
- Logo: SolDirectory logo, white version on navy and black version on white (I will upload both). Keep clear space around it.
- Look: calm, trustworthy, plain, lots of white space, rounded corners, simple line icons (a tick, a map pin, a calendar, a shield).
- Pictures: NO photographs of people and no stock photos of carers. Use only shapes, icons and simple illustrations built from the brand colours.
  Every graphic needs one line of alt text.

WHAT TO PRODUCE
1. Three subject lines (under 45 characters each), plus a 90-character preview text for each. Friendly, honest, no capitals-shouting, no
   exclamation marks, no emojis. Subject lines may mention claiming a free listing; they must not promise results.
2. ONE email, 120 to 160 words, addressed to the business by name using the Mailchimp merge tag *|MMERGE2|* for the business name.
   Structure: one-sentence reason for writing (their business is on the public register and has a page on SolDirectory) / what a
   free claim gives them (use only the facts above) / one button: "Claim your free listing" linking to
   https://directory.solbusinessconsultant.com.au/providers / a one-line "not affiliated with the NDIA or NDIS Commission" note /
   a human sign-off from "The SolDirectory team".
3. The email footer, ready to paste: why they received it (their business email is published on their website and on the public
   register), Sol Business Consultant Pty Ltd, ABN 20 662 022 522, 97 John St, Glenroy VIC 3046, the contact details, and the unsubscribe
   link as *|UNSUB|*.
4. A design brief for the email header image, 600 pixels wide by 200 tall: layout, text on it (max 8 words), colours, icons.
5. THREE social graphics for LinkedIn and Facebook, each with the exact words on the image (max 12 words), colours, icon, layout
   and sizes: 1200 x 627 (LinkedIn/Facebook link post), 1080 x 1080 (square), 1080 x 1350 (portrait). Plus a 2-line caption for each
   that ends with the link above. Give 5 hashtags that are plain and relevant (for example #NDIS #AgedCare #DisabilitySupport).
6. A step-by-step list for building the email in Mailchimp as a complete beginner (import the CSV, map the columns, make the template,
   merge tags, send a test to myself, check the unsubscribe link works, schedule in small batches).
7. A 5-point checklist I can tick before pressing Send, covering the three legal rules above and the honesty rules.

CSV COLUMNS I WILL IMPORT: Email Address, Business Name (map to MMERGE2), Register, State, Suburb, Support categories, Website.
Offer ONE optional tweak per state or register type that uses the State or Register column (for example "NDIS" vs "Aged care"), without
inventing any facts.

Write everything in Australian English. Keep the whole answer practical and copy-paste ready.
```

## What to do with what you get back

1. **Read the email out loud.** If any sentence promises results or sounds like a guarantee, delete it. When unsure, ask us.
2. **Check every fact against the "About SolDirectory" list above.** If the AI added something that is not in that list (a number, a feature, a
   claim about "leads"), remove it.
3. **Build the graphics in Canva** (free account is enough): custom sizes from the prompt, Barlow font, our two logo files, colours typed in as hex codes.
4. **Import the CSV into Mailchimp** (Audience > Import contacts > Upload a file > map "Business Name" to a new text field and keep
   "Email Address"). Tick that you have permission only after we have confirmed the list in writing.
5. **Send yourself a test** and open it on a phone. Click the button and the unsubscribe link.
6. **Send in small batches**, check Mailchimp's report the next day, and send us the "Unsubscribed" and "Cleaned" lists.

If anything here is unclear, ask before sending. Nothing is urgent enough to risk a complaint or a blocked account.

## Files and commands (for the developer)

- Export the list (on the server, where the live database is): `npm run export:outreach -w apps/api` writes `apps/api/exports/outreach-verified-<date>.csv`.
  Add `-- --count` to see numbers only; `-- --type ndis|aged_care` or `-- --state VIC` to narrow; `-- --batch-size 300` adds a "Batch" column
  (Batch 001, Batch 002, ...) so one batch can be sent a day. The script cleans every field, then reads the file back and prints
  "Check passed" or says what is wrong. Open the file in Google Sheets or import it straight into Mailchimp; Excel is not needed. `-- --include-unverified` adds addresses not matched to the
  business's own website; use it only after a lawyer or the owner has agreed.
- Feed unsubscribes back before the next send: `npm run import:outreach-optouts -w apps/api -- <mailchimp-unsubscribed-export.csv>`.
- Delete the exported CSV from the server once the campaign is loaded.
